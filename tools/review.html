How to label

  1. Box every real person you can see - standing, sitting, seen from straight above, partly hidden behind a monitor, a desk or another person. If only a head and shoulders show, box what shows.
  2. One box per person. Delete the extras: a second box on the same person, a small box on just the head inside a body box, one box spanning two people (draw two instead).
  3. Not a person: pictures and posters (the printed woman on the carton in Camera 3), reflections in glass, empty chairs, a coat on a chair. Delete those boxes - the model learns from that too.
  4. Phone (orange, key 2): a mobile phone you can tell is a phone - in a hand, at an ear, on a desk. Skip it if you can't tell.
  5. Boxes go snug around the visible part. Dashed boxes are guesses the model was unsure about (score under 0.35) - those need the closest look.
  6. Grey areas are the app's ignore areas. Still box real people inside them.
  7. Exclude (X) a broken frame: grey, smeared or half decoded.
  8. On the sheet, Correct under a picture saves it as it is. Only use it when every box is right and nobody is missing.
