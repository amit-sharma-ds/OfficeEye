Sleeping / Head down

People on the counting cameras who sit still with their head down - on the desk, on their arms or hanging forward - for a while. A quick look down at the desk does not count. The minutes and the times are set under Rules & timing.

When does it count?

minutes of sitting still with the head down

The person has to stay still with the head no higher than the shoulders this long. Somebody who looks up or gets moving starts again from zero.

How it is checked

Every check, each person's picture is compared with the last one - somebody typing or talking changes it, somebody asleep barely does. Only people who have been still for a minute are looked at by a small pose model, which finds the head and shoulders. Ceiling cameras see heads from above, so now and then somebody reading with the head bent may be listed - the photo shows which.
