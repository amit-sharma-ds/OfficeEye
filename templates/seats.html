Monitoring agent

Every seat on the cameras you switch on: who sat there, from when to when, and for how long - with a photo and a short clip of each arrival, so the history of every seat can be looked back at. Seats find themselves: where somebody stays put for a minute or two becomes a seat. It runs on the checks the cameras already get - no extra model time.

Nobody is recognised by face: who sat is what the photo and the clip show (the colour of the clothes helps to tell one sitting from the next). Tell the staff that the seats are watched. Clips are kept on the office PC with the photos.

Draw seats

Not needed on a camera that is on - its seats find themselves. Here they can be named (a desk number or whose seat it is), moved, deleted, or drawn by hand: drag a box over a seat - the chair and where a person sitting on it shows (head and shoulders too). Drag a box to move it, its bottom-right corner to resize it. Suggest puts a box on every chair and person this camera sees now. Then Save - saving also switches the camera on. A seat you delete stays deleted: its place is marked not a seat (red) and is never found again by itself.
