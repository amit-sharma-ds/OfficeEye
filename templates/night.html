Night vision

After the office has closed, the counting cameras and the washroom-door camera watch for anybody in the building: a person, any movement (even where it is too dark to make out who), and lights switched on or off - each with photos. Most cameras switch to infrared (black and white) in the dark; one without infrared sees nothing then.

When does the night watch run?

to next morning

The office closes in the evening; anybody seen between these times is recorded.

minutes

A person is always recorded - this only keeps movement from flooding the list. To keep a TV or a fan out, mark it with Ignore areas on the camera's live view.
